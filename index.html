Our Mission

To demonstrate the love of God through outreach initiatives that offer spiritual guidance, encouragement and serve a belonging to young people, facilitating life's challenges.

Our Programs

Community Outreach

To reach out to young people through faith-based community and school outreaches.

Charity Campaigns

To support less advantaged children in communities through giving them basic school requirements.

Preaching

To provide spiritual mentorship and encouragement to those with limited support system.

Spiritual Content

Scripture 1

SALVATION THROUGH LOVE. We believe we are saved by Grace through faith in Jesus. This new life compels us to love God and our neighbors. Ephesians 2:8-9, 1 John 4:19.

Scripture 2

8. THE CHURCH AS A COMMUNITY OF LOVE. We believe the church is a family of friends of Jesus who obey his command to love, encourage and build one another up. John 15:14, Hebrews 10:24-25.
